# Build notes

I built this handheld in July–August 2024, adapting the PiGRRL 2 guide to a Pi 4B, 5-inch screen, and different power supply.

## Getting everything to fit

The enclosure took more attempts than I expected. I worked in Meshmixer and printed about ten versions, but each was still slightly too small when I tried assembling the handheld. I ended up using hot glue to join the sides.

That made the handheld usable, but it left room for improvement. The enclosure had to fit the full assembly, including the wiring and the space needed to close it. The saved [Meshmixer files](../cad/) are part of that work, though I haven't matched them to a complete sequence of printed revisions.

## Display

The larger screen initially looked very pixelated. Getting it to cooperate took some troubleshooting. I don't remember the setting or change that fixed it, so I haven't added instructions that might point someone to the wrong solution.

## Power and audio

I used a different power supply from the original guide and assembled the battery, wiring, speaker, and amplifier inside the housing. The build photos show how those parts were arranged. I don't have the exact power-board specifications or battery-runtime measurements.

## Heat and the next build

I overlooked ventilation, and the handheld overheated inside the enclosure. I didn't correct it at the time. When I built the Pi 5 version, I added vents and copper heatsinks.

There are no temperature logs from this build. The overheating and fit problems are what I remember from using and assembling it, supported by the photos and design files where available.
